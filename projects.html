Photonic Ising Machine

TFLN Mach-Zehnder modulators, SOAs, and an RFSoC 4x2 FPGA, with optical matrix-vector multiplication validated at 6.12 effective bits. A discrete, constraint-aware Ising solver reaches ground-state convergence on all 23 benchmark problems. Undergraduate thesis.

Long-Range Correlations in a Driven Ising Chain

Tuning the drive period of a transverse-field Ising chain makes correlations between excitations arbitrarily long-ranged, shown analytically and numerically. Preprint on arXiv, 2026. The strip above is a live view of this system.

2026 to 2027 · ENPH 454 capstone

Near-Infrared Medical Vein Finder

A device that images veins in near-infrared light, which veins absorb more than skin. IR LED illumination, a cross-polarized camera to remove glare, and a Raspberry Pi that locates the veins and projects the map back onto the arm.

Nanowire Quantum Dot Single-Photon Sources

Undergraduate Research Award in Prof. Michael Reimer’s QPD Lab. Emission spectra from 100+ nanowire quantum dots fitted with a phonon-sideband model, entanglement-swapping experiments, and FDTD simulation of near-unity absorption.
